<div class="home-bio">
  <p>I am a master’s student at <strong>Shanghai Jiao Tong University</strong>. My research lies at the intersection of generative AI, multimodal learning, and computer vision, with a focus on building expressive and interactive digital humans.</p>

  <p>I work on 3D facial motion generation, animatable avatars, and multimodal generative modeling. I am also interested in intelligent agents that can understand long-horizon context, track evolving goals, and provide timely, useful assistance.</p>

  <p>My current work explores how structured inductive biases and long-term contextual modeling can make generated behavior more realistic, controllable, and useful in real-world interaction.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-grid" aria-label="Research focus">
  <section class="research-item">
    <h3>Conversational Digital Humans</h3>
    <p>Generating facial motion that preserves speech articulation while capturing the diversity of interactive reactions.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="research-item">
    <h3>3D Avatars</h3>

  </section>

  <section class="research-item">
    <h3>Long-Horizon Intelligent Agents</h3>
    <p>Designing agents that maintain causal context, recognize useful opportunities, and act with appropriate timing.</p>
  </section>
</div>
