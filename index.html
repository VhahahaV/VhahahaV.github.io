Zhuoqiang Cai

M.S. Student in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University

I build generative models for interactive digital humans and intelligent agents.

I am a master’s student in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research lies at the intersection of generative AI, multimodal learning, and computer vision, with a focus on expressive and interactive digital humans.

Before graduate study, I completed my bachelor’s education in Software Engineering at SJTU and interned at Taobao and Tmall Group, Alibaba Group. Alongside research, I enjoy turning ideas into useful desktop software through vibe coding.

I work on 3D facial motion generation, animatable avatars, and multimodal generative modeling. I am also interested in intelligent agents that can understand long-horizon context and provide timely, useful assistance.

Conversational Digital Humans

Generating facial motion that preserves speech articulation while capturing the diversity of interactive reactions.

3D Avatars

Reconstructing high-fidelity, animatable head avatars from lightweight, in-the-wild observations.

Long-Horizon Intelligent Agents

Designing agents that maintain causal context, recognize useful opportunities, and act with appropriate timing.

Selected Publications

  1. ACM MM
    ECHO: Dyadic 3D Facial Motion Generation with Asymmetric Deterministic Articulation and Stochastic Reaction
    Zhuoqiang Cai, Yujie Sun, Chaoyue Niu, Hongyun Yu, Zhiwen Chen, Chengfei Lv, and Fan Wu
    In Proceedings of the 34th ACM International Conference on Multimedia, Nov 2026
    Abs DOI Bib HTML PDF

    ECHO models dyadic 3D facial motion from dual-stream conversational audio by separating stable speech-related articulation from diverse listener reactions through a deterministic anchor and stochastic residual.

  2. CVPR
    FHAvatar: Fast and High-Fidelity Reconstruction of Face-and-Hair Composable 3D Head Avatar from Few Casual Captures
    Yujie Sun, Zhuoqiang Cai, Chaoyue Niu, Jianchuan Chen, Zhiwen Chen, Chengfei Lv, and Fan Wu
    In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    FHAvatar reconstructs composable face-and-hair 3D Gaussian head avatars from only a few casual captures, enabling fast personalization, real-time animation, hairstyle transfer, and stylized editing.
