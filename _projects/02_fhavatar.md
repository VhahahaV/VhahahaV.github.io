<div class="project-lead">
  <p><strong>FHAvatar</strong> reconstructs high-fidelity, animatable 3D head avatars from only a few casual captures. It explicitly models face and hair as composable components, allowing each region to use a representation suited to its geometry and appearance.</p>
</div>

An aggregated transformer backbone learns cross-view priors from multi-view data, enabling fast personalization for new identities. The resulting avatar supports real-time animation, hairstyle transfer, and stylized editing.

### Highlights

- **Composable representation:** face and hair are modeled as distinct but coordinated components.
- **Fast personalization:** a learned cross-view prior supports reconstruction from sparse casual observations.
- **Interactive use:** the avatar supports real-time animation, hairstyle transfer, and stylized editing.
